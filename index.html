The benchmark

Five pillars. One clearer picture.

Great games are more than graphics or review scores. V.E.G.A.S. looks at the broader experience - from launch practices to long-term technical health.

01 / V

Values

Awards points for reasonable launch pricing, monetization, access models, and advertising pressure.

02 / E

Ethics

Checks marketing honesty, feature delivery, and long-term developer support.

03 / G

Gameplay

Scores points for campaign depth, runtime, replayability, and meaningful player choice.

04 / A

Accessibility

Kudos for input support, visual options, ease-of-use features, and localization.

05 / S

Standards

Big W's for offline access, community freedom, technical health, and launch reception.

Built for trust

A benchmark you can actually understand.

V.E.G.A.S. is designed around explicit criteria rather than a mysterious single number. The goal is not to tell you what to like - it is to make important differences between games easier to see.
