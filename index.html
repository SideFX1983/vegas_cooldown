About V.E.G.A.S. Cooldowns

Committed to elevating the industry through uncompromising standards. Our mission is to challenge publishers to reach higher and to offer players a trusted benchmark of excellence. A benchmark around Values, Ethics, Gameplay, Accessibility, and Standards.

Value

Launch pricing, monetization, access models, and ad pressure.

Ethics

Marketing honesty, feature delivery, and long-term developer support.

Gameplay

Campaign depth, runtime, replayability, and meaningful player choice.

Accessibility

Input support, visual options, ease-of-use features, and localization.

Standards

Offline access, community freedom, technical health, and launch reception.
